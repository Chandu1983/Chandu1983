# 🔭 SNAP Astro Dashboard (Multi-Star Version)

An exploratory, data-driven framework optimized for tracking structural deviations, boundary instabilities, and critical transition regimes across dynamical astrophysical configurations.

---

## 🔬 Core Scientific Principle
This project implements a scale-free and parameter-free **Dimensionless Boundary Index ($\Pi_I$)**. Rather than asserting a new physical invariant constant or challenging established laws, this framework serves as a phenomenological template to mathematically observe and evaluate state deviations within dynamical systems.

The underlying engine operates on the fundamental interaction balance:
$$\Pi_I = \Pi_B - \Pi_T$$

Where:
* **$\Pi_B$ (Vacuum Pressure / Energy Density Scale):** Represents the normalized local energy density ($\rho_{\text{local}} / \rho_{\text{ref}}$) of the system.
* **$\Pi_T$ (Vacuum Tension / Geometric Scale):** Represents the curvature-based geometric term driven by first-derivative transitions ($1.0 + \alpha \cdot dS$).
* **$\Pi_I$ (Interaction Index):** Tracks boundary anomalies. Under perfect vacuum equilibrium, $T = B$, leading to $\Pi_I = 0$.

## 🎯 The Golden "Peak-to-Decline" Rule
The engine detects critical **"SNAP" events** (boundary balance disruptions). Empirically, these structural transitions do not trigger during mid-quiescence or random noise. A verified **SNAP Event** is locked precisely at the turning point where a dynamical configuration reaches its *peak intensity/brightness* and begins its initial phase of decline. 

At this transition boundary, internal pressure ($\Pi_B$) and geometric tension ($\Pi_T$) decouple, producing a sharp positive spike captured seamlessly by our Bayesian probability matrix.
